The MPSTME Library stands at the heart of the Institute, facilitating teaching, learning, research, and extension programmes for the Institute. Fittingly located on the 7th floor, the well-furnished and air-conditioned library is spread over 5,646 square feet. The library takes pride in stocking all classic and updated volumes alongside the most prestigious journals and publications in Engineering, Technology, and Management. The library also houses a collection of books on mental health awareness and soft skills ensuring our students are equipped to manage their mental and emotional wellbeing.
The library is fully automated with KOHA software. Users can log in freely to a high-speed Wi-Fi network to access the e-resources. Web-OPAC, currently on the intranet, gives detailed information about library books. Users can find the real-time availability of library materials from their computer terminals. The library has an open-access system facilitating the unrestricted use of materials on the shelves. The library has a rich collection of books on management, engineering, and allied subjects and periodicals (National/International), audio-visual materials, and e-resources.
