A Transcript is the consolidated document or a summary about the academic information of the student for a given programme. The said transcript will have information about the programme pursued by a student, year of enrolment, year of completion, duration of the Program, the Medium of instructions etc.It also contains the information as regards to courses/ subjects offered in each trimester/ semester in an academic year with the credits earned for each subject, grades obtained by the student, Grade Point Average after each trimester/semester, Cumulative Grade Point Average till date. A Transcript also gives additional explanation of the credit structure of the University and is issued on University Letterhead and it is the authenticate document issued by the competent authority of the University.

B. FAQs (Frequently Asked Questions)
The “SRB” (Student’s Resource Book), is the document which provides the information, guidelines and rules related to academics, attendance, examination, library, placement, and day to day functioning of the school. All students are bound by the rules mentioned in SRB.
How the students will get information on Final/Re-Exam dates/time table?
Information about Final/Re-examination dates are available in the Academic Calendar of the Student Resource Book of the respective school of every academic year. The Academic calendar is also shared by the Academic dept at the beginning of Academic year. The Final/Re- exam time-table is published approximately 30 – 45 days prior to the commencement of examination. The student can view this time table on the Student Portal as well as on the NMIMS website (https://nmims.edu/examination).
Who can apply for re-examination?
Student who was Absent/Fail or score D grade in Final /Re- Exam can apply for re-exam. Read the Promotion/Progression rule given in SRB to understand the eligble number of attempts the student can appear for a particular semester/trimester re-examination.
What if a student does not apply for the re-examination in the given time period? Is there any provision of application by collecting extra fee?
There is no provision for apply for re-examination after the registration window is closed as per the time slot given in the Re-Examination time table. In such case, the student concerned lose the chance to appear for the re-examination.
There is no provision for applying for re-examination after the given time slot is over. In such case, the student concerned stands to lose the chance to appear at the re-examination.
