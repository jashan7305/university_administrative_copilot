For Technical Program / Courses :
A full refund of fees will be provided for cancellation of admission to technical courses / programs on or before 20th July 2026, as per AICTE norms.
For Non- Technical Program / Courses :
Refund is as per UGC refund policy 2024-25, (dated 12th June 2024, bearing the number D.O. No.F.2-7 1t2022 (CPP-Il) (c-1 14s46)) or subsequent amendment, if any.

Cancellation policy not valid for online and distance learning program (NCDOE School)
The student who wishes to withdraw admission should fill the application form using the url https://nmims.edu/admission-cancellation available on our website nmims.edu. Withdrawal request can be applied only through this link.
Request before commencement of program: the admission department will process the document with the necessary approvals.
Request post the commencement of program: the candidate will have to get the Clearance /No dues from all the concerned departments of the School.
Once the process is completed, the refund of the fees (if applicable) shall be processed within 15 days of submission of the completed form with associated documents. Refund of fees, if any, will be credited to the bank account details provided by the applicant in the application. If any dues are to be collected, that will be deducted before a refund of the fees.
The process to be followed is as below:
- The student will submit the application form available on our website.
- The admission office will check the completion of the application and process if it is complete.
- If the application is post commencement the candidate will have to provide the clearance/ no dues from different departments.
- Documents will be sent for approval and then submitted to the Accounts department.
- It will take 15 days for refund (if applicable). Kindly note, in case of pandemic going on and increasing or natural calamity we will inform you of any delay
- Candidate can put a request for Migration / leaving certificate if the withdrawal is post commencement of the program.
