MPSTME library stands at the heart of the Institute, facilitating teaching, learning, research, and extension programs of the Institute. Conveniently located on the 7th floor, the well-furnished and air-conditioned library is spread over 5,646 square feet. The library takes pride in stocking all classic and updated volumes alongside the most prestigious journals and publications in Engineering, Technology, and Management. The immersive silence in the library reading area resonates with the quickening of young minds with new knowledge and often poignant questions that provide the stimulus for research and discovery.
The NMIMS Deemed to be University hostel facility includes two full-fledged hostels and three rented residential accommodations (taken care of by the NMIMS University) in Mumbai., Preference is given to students from cities other than Mumbai.
Students are treated with good quality and affordable food in the hostel mess that ensures that food is the least of the worries of the budding engineers of tomorrow.
