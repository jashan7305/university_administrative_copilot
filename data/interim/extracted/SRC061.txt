STATEMENT OF MARKS (PERCENTAGE LETTER)
Dear Students,
With a view to help the students of NMIMS in obtaining the Statement of Marks (Percentage Letter) in a fast and seamless manner, we have decided to provide the students a facility of ‘Electronic Delivery of Statement of Marks (Percentage Letter)'.
This will ensure;
- Easy and speedy process to deliver Statement of Marks (Percentage Letter) to students
- Students need not visit NMIMS University for making an application for Statement of Marks (Percentage Letter) and payment of fees. Application and payment would be online.
- Secure and fast payment of fees through payment gateway
- Student will receive a copy of Statement of Marks (Percentage Letter) approved by official of NMIMS by email
- Fast delivery of Statement of Marks (Percentage Letter) compared to traditional mode (courier / personal collection)
- Help desk facility available for any assistance
- Save valuable time and money
