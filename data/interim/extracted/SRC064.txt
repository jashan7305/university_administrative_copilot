We at SVKM’s NMIMS University aim to foster a space of holistic development and growth for all our students. In order to take care of their emotional needs and wellbeing we have two trained and experienced psychologists available on campus.
Counselling is a space to discuss your concerns with a qualified and experienced professional in a non-judgemental and confidential environment.

NMIMS Counselling Team recognizes that emotional difficulties in any area of life may result in an impact on your academics and career progression. Keeping this in mind we have an open door policy and warmly invite students to reach out whenever required.
If you notice disturbances in your thoughts/feelings/behaviour, we urge you to proactively reach out for support. Early intervention and preventing emotional distress from escalation and can lead to better outcomes.
We operate on the principles of complete and total confidentiality. Any information you trust us with, is completely safeguarded, only excluding risk to life situations.
You can connect with our Counsellor (Psychologist) for absolutely any concerns you may be experiencing.
- Life transition and adjustment issues
- Anxiety, Stress or Depression
- Loneliness and Peer Pressure
- Self-worth and low self esteem
- Relationship difficulties/Family conflicts
- Personal growth and skill development
- Academic challenges
- Addictive behaviours
- Grief and Loss
No problem is too small to be addressed. No challenge is too big to be resolved.
Come as you are; this is a safe space.
Timings: Monday to Friday – 10 am to 5.30 pm
Saturday (2nd and 4th) – 10 am to 5.30 pm
You can reach out to us (via call or email) to make appointments for any challenges you may face throughout your academic year.
