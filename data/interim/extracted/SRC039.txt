NMIMS – Deemed-to-be University is one of India’s finest multidisciplinary educational 
institutions, imparting quality higher education. The University spans across locations in India, 
namely Mumbai, Bengaluru, Dhule, Indore, Hyderabad, Navi Mumbai, Shirpu r and Chandigarh. 
NMIMS has earned great distinction and recognition for offering world -class education, with 
courses at par with leading international institutions and state-of-the-art teaching pedagogy, so as 
to groom and develop well -rounded professionals and leaders. The student community is highly 
motivated, pro -active and receptive. It gives us great pleasure to announce that since 2016, a 
Student Council body at the University level has been established and this year too, a new vibrant 
NMIMS University Students’ Council (NUSC) has been formed. The primary objective of this is 
to assimilate and integrate students from all the constituents and schools across various campuses 
in Mumbai and other locations and to provide the students with a platform to harness their creative 
activities. The NUSC (NMIMS University Students’ Council) proposes to promote collective and 
constructive leadership within the student community. We are proud to introduce to you the NUSC 
team for 2025-26:
