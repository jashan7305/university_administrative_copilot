MPSTME management recognizes that a progressive institute is always differentiated through its sound administrative practices. Also a well designed administrative network that is well aligned with the philosophy of the University is instrumental in taking the institute towards realization of its vision. MPSTME administration Comprises of stalwarts and leaders who are deeply intuned to MPSTME vision and aptly carry forward the NMIMS legacy. Administrative practices at MPSTME rest firmly on the principle of a flat hierarchical network and open door policy. The Administrative support staff is trained and encouraged to ensure a smooth and homogenous transition of students into the institute. Students are guided and supported throughout to ensure that their trajectory with us remains hassle free and memorable in every aspect.
