WES Process
Dear Students,
WES and NMIMS have enabled an option for students to send their Academic documents to WES electronically. This is to facilitate fast, secure and seamless transfer of their documents (transcripts, degrees, and statements of marks) from NMIMS to WES.
1. Please click on the link below to view the process to be followed
2. WES does not accept documents sent directly by students or any other person except NMIMS
3. Please do not send documents by post or courier to WES. Follow the process of sending your documents through electronically to WES.
