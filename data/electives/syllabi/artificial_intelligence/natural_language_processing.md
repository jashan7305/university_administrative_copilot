Course Outline

Course Code: CE-PE-614
Course Title: Natural Language Processing
Course Type: Program Elective
Program(s): B Tech Computer Engineering; B Tech Information Technology
Year / Semester: III / VI
Credits: 3 (Lecture 2, Practical 2, Tutorial 0)
Prerequisites: Machine Learning; Probability and Statistics

Course Objectives
1. Introduce the computational treatment of human language.
2. Build text classification, tagging and parsing systems with classical and neural methods.

Course Outcomes
CO1 Pre-process and represent text (tokenisation, n-grams, TF-IDF, word embeddings).
CO2 Build sequence labelling models for part-of-speech tagging and named entity recognition.
CO3 Apply transformer encoders to classification and question answering.

Detailed Syllabus
Unit 1  Text processing: tokenisation, morphology, regular expressions, edit distance.
Unit 2  Language models: n-gram models, smoothing, perplexity.
Unit 3  Text classification: naive Bayes, logistic regression, evaluation.
Unit 4  Sequence labelling: HMMs, CRFs, named entity recognition.
Unit 5  Word embeddings and transformers: word2vec, attention, BERT fine-tuning.

Text Books: Jurafsky and Martin, Speech and Language Processing (3rd ed. draft).
