Course Outline

Course Code: AI-PE-721
Course Title: Reinforcement Learning
Course Type: Program Elective
Program(s): B Tech Artificial Intelligence; B Tech Computer Science
Year / Semester: IV / VII
Credits: 3 (Lecture 2, Practical 2, Tutorial 0)
Prerequisites: Deep Learning; Probability and Statistics

Detailed Syllabus
Unit 1  Markov decision processes, returns, value functions, Bellman equations.
Unit 2  Dynamic programming, Monte Carlo and temporal-difference learning.
Unit 3  Function approximation, deep Q-networks.
Unit 4  Policy gradients, actor-critic methods, PPO.
Unit 5  Applications: games, robotics, recommendation.
