Course Outline

Course Code: CS-PE-512
Course Title: Deep Learning
Course Type: Program Elective
Program(s): B Tech Computer Science; B Tech Computer Engineering; B Tech Information Technology; B Tech Computer Science and Business Systems
Year / Semester: III / V
Credits: 4 (Lecture 3, Practical 2, Tutorial 0)
Prerequisites: Machine Learning; Linear Algebra

Course Objectives
1. Explain how deep neural networks are trained and regularised.
2. Design convolutional and recurrent architectures for vision and sequence data.

Detailed Syllabus
Unit 1  Neural networks: perceptron, multilayer networks, backpropagation, activation functions.
Unit 2  Optimisation: SGD, momentum, Adam, batch normalisation, dropout, weight decay.
Unit 3  Convolutional networks: convolution, pooling, ResNet, transfer learning.
Unit 4  Sequence models: RNN, LSTM, GRU, sequence-to-sequence learning.
Unit 5  Practical deep learning: PyTorch, GPUs, experiment tracking.
