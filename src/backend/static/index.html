Interaction with another group's component

The advisor can pull syllabi from a partner syllabus service and the academic identity from a partner student-record service. Any service that follows the contract plugs in with one setting (PARTNER_SYLLABUS_URL, PARTNER_STUDENT_URL). Until then a mock with the same contract answers.

Contract

GET {PARTNER_SYLLABUS_URL}/syllabi?topic=...
 -> {"courses": [{"course_id", "title",
                  "syllabus_text"}]}
GET {PARTNER_STUDENT_URL}/students/{id}
 -> {"program", "year", "semester",
     "completed_courses": [...]}

Only programme, year, semester and completed courses are requested: no names, contact details or grades.

Responsible AI, in short

  • Advice, not a decision. Every result says the course coordinator confirms the enrolment, and lists what they should check.
  • Explained and sourced. Each check names its rule: an NMIMS rule (with page), the syllabus, or our own advising policy.
  • No personal attributes used. Only programme, year, semester, completed courses and optional interests. Adding name, gender, religion, caste or region to the text does not change the advice; this is tested.
  • Knows its limits. Unknown programme or missing details: no recommendation, "needs review" instead.
  • Nothing stored. Requests are not logged or kept.
  • Coverage. Only MPSTME programme structures (2026) are in the catalogue; the sample syllabi are illustrative.
