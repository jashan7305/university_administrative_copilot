# Results, Answer Sheet Photocopy and Revaluation

## Results
End semester results are declared on the exam portal within 45 days of the last paper. Internal assessment marks are published by the department before the end semester exams.

## Photocopy of the answer sheet
To see your evaluated answer sheet, apply for a photocopy within 7 days of the result (Rs. 300 per paper).

## Revaluation
Apply on form EX-21 within 7 days of the result (or of receiving the photocopy) and pay Rs. 700 per paper. A different examiner re-evaluates the paper, and the revised result is published within 30 days. Marks may go up or down.

## Result errors
If the result shows you absent for a paper you attended, report it to the Results Section with your hall ticket and seat number within 7 days.
