# Failed Payments, Double Charges, Receipts and Refunds

## Money deducted but payment failed
If money was debited but the portal shows the fee as unpaid, wait 24 hours. Most such transactions are reversed by the bank in 5 to 7 working days. If the fee is still pending after 24 hours, email the transaction ID (UTR number) and a bank statement to the Accounts Office helpdesk.

## Charged twice
Duplicate payments are refunded to the source account after verification, usually within 15 working days.

## Receipts
Fee receipts can be downloaded from the portal (Fees > Receipts). If a receipt shows the wrong amount or name, raise a ticket with the Accounts Office.

## Refund on withdrawal
Students who cancel admission before the semester starts get a full refund minus Rs. 1,000 processing charges. After the start, refunds follow the UGC refund policy. Apply on form AC-05 with a cancelled cheque. Refunds are processed within 30 days.
