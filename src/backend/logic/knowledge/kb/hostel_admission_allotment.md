# Hostel Admission and Room Allotment

## Eligibility
First-year students whose permanent address is more than 50 km from campus are given priority. Senior students are allotted rooms based on availability and academic standing.

## Procedure
1. Apply on the hostel portal with form HS-01, stating your room preference (shared, double occupancy or single, AC or non-AC).
2. Upload the admission letter, a medical fitness certificate, photographs and the parent undertaking.
3. The allotment list is published on the hostel notice board and portal within 10 days of the deadline.
4. Pay the hostel fee and the refundable security deposit of Rs. 10,000 within 7 days of allotment. Otherwise the room goes to the waiting list.

## Room change
Room change requests are accepted in the first two weeks of each semester on form HS-03, subject to availability, with the warden's approval.
