# Fee Concessions and Waivers

The university offers partial fee concessions to students with family income below Rs. 8 lakh per year, siblings studying at the university (10% concession for the younger sibling), and wards of university staff.

## Procedure
Apply on form AC-09 with a current income certificate and your previous grade card. A committee of the Accounts Office and Scholarship Cell decides within 21 days. Concessions are adjusted in the next fee challan.

## Note
Full fee waivers under government schemes are handled by the Scholarship Cell. See the government scholarship procedure.
