# Need-Based Aid, Sports Scholarships and Research Fellowships

## Need-based financial aid
Students with family income below Rs. 4.5 lakh per year can receive up to 75% tuition support from the university's financial aid fund. An interview with the Scholarship Committee may be required.

## Sports scholarship
Students who have represented the state or country, or won medals at inter-university events, are eligible for fee waivers of 25% to 100%. Certificates must be verified by the Sports Officer.

## Research fellowships
Undergraduate research fellowships (Rs. 10,000 per semester) are offered to students working on faculty-supervised projects. Apply with a proposal endorsed by the supervisor.

## Appeals
Rejected applicants can appeal to the Dean of Student Welfare within 15 days of the decision.
