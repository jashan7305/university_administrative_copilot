# Attendance Requirement, Shortage and Condonation

## Minimum attendance
Students need at least 75% attendance in each course to be eligible for the end semester examination. Students below 75% are detained from that course's exam and must repeat it.

## Condonation
A shortage of up to 10% (attendance between 65% and 75%) can be condoned by the Head of Department on valid grounds: medical illness, representing the university in sports or cultural events, or a family emergency. Apply on form AT-02 with supporting documents and pay a Rs. 500 condonation fee. Attendance below 65% cannot be condoned.

## Deadlines
Condonation applications must be submitted before the examination form deadline.
