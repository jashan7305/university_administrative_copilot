# Provisional Certificate, Degree Certificate, Corrections and Duplicates

The provisional degree certificate is issued after final results are declared and remains valid until the degree certificate is awarded at convocation. It also serves as a course completion certificate.

## Provisional certificate
Apply on the examination portal after the final semester result, pay Rs. 300 and collect it in 5 working days.

## Degree certificate
Awarded at convocation. Students who cannot attend may apply for the degree in absentia, and it will be posted to their registered address.

## Corrections
Spelling mistakes in the name or other details are corrected on submission of the original certificate, the 10th standard certificate as proof, and a fee of Rs. 500.

## Duplicates
A duplicate degree certificate requires an FIR or police lost report, a notarised affidavit and a fee of Rs. 2,000. It is issued in about 30 days after verification.
