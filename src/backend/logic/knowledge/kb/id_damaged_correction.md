# Damaged ID Card, Corrections and Photo Update

## Damaged or non-working cards
If the card is cracked, the barcode does not scan, or the RFID chip does not work at the gates or library, surrender the card with form AD-08. The fee is Rs. 150 if the damage is physical. If the chip is faulty within the first year, the card is replaced free.

## Corrections
Errors in the name, programme, roll number or date of birth are corrected free of charge when the error was the university's. Attach proof such as the admission letter.

## Photo update
Submit a recent photograph with form AD-08. The card is reprinted in 3 working days.
