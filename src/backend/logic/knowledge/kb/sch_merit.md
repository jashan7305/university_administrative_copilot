# Merit Scholarship

The university awards merit scholarships to the top 5% of students in each programme and year, based on the previous academic year's CGPA. Department toppers receive a 50% tuition fee waiver, and other awardees receive Rs. 25,000 per year.

## Eligibility
A minimum CGPA of 8.5 in the previous year, no backlogs, and no disciplinary action.

## Procedure
1. Apply on the scholarship portal with form SC-01 when the circular is issued (usually in August).
2. Upload your grade card, bank passbook and Aadhaar card.
3. The list of awardees is published about 45 days after the deadline. The amount is credited directly to your bank account.

## Renewal
The scholarship is renewed each year if you maintain a CGPA of 8.0 or above. It can be held alongside an education loan, but not alongside another university-funded scholarship.
