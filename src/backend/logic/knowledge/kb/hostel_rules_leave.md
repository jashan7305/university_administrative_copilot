# Hostel Rules, Leave and Night-Out Permission

## Timings
Hostel gates close at 22:30 on weekdays and 23:00 on weekends. Late entry is recorded, and three late entries in a month are reported to parents.

## Leave and night-out
Weekend leave and night-outs require an application on the hostel app at least 24 hours in advance, with parent consent through the app. Leave during examinations needs the warden's approval.

## Visitors
Visitors are allowed in the common room between 16:00 and 19:00 with a valid ID. Overnight guests are not permitted.

## Vacation stay
Students who need to stay during the summer or winter vacation (for projects, internships or exams) must apply on form HS-05 two weeks before the vacation, with a recommendation from their department. Mess charges during the vacation are billed per day.

## Appliances
Cooking appliances and heaters are not allowed in rooms. Kettles and laptops are permitted.
