# Migration Certificate and Transfer Certificate (TC / Leaving Certificate)

A migration certificate allows you to join another university after completing or leaving your programme. A transfer (leaving) certificate records your conduct and the date you left the institution. Both are usually requested together.

## Eligibility
Students who have completed the programme, or who are leaving mid-way after formally withdrawing admission.

## Procedure
1. Obtain the no dues certificate signed by the library, hostel office, department and accounts office.
2. Fill form SS-04 on the portal, choosing migration, transfer or both.
3. Surrender the original ID card and pay Rs. 500.
4. The certificates are issued in 7 working days. Collect them in person or authorise a representative with a signed letter and a copy of your photo ID.

## Notes
The migration certificate is issued only once. A duplicate requires an affidavit and an FIR copy in case of loss, and costs Rs. 1,000.
