# Attendance Corrections, Medical Leave and Duty Leave

## Checking attendance
Course-wise attendance is updated on the student portal each week (Academics > Attendance).

## Corrections
If you were marked absent but were present, or the professor has not updated attendance, first contact the course faculty. If it is not resolved within 7 days, submit form AT-01 to the department office.

## Medical leave
Medical leave must be reported within 3 days of returning, with a medical certificate. It is counted during condonation but not marked as present.

## Duty leave
Students representing the university at fests, hackathons, sports events or conferences get duty leave, which counts as present. The event coordinator must submit the list to the department in advance. Guest lectures and department activities are counted as attendance only if the faculty takes attendance.

## Faculty upload
Faculty upload attendance through the faculty portal. Bulk upload for a batch is done with the Excel template.
