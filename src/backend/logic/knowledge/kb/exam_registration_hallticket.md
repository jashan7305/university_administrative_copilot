# Examination Form, Timetable and Hall Ticket

## Examination form
The end semester examination form opens on the exam portal about six weeks before the exams. Fill it before the deadline and pay Rs. 1,500. A late fee of Rs. 500 applies for one week after the deadline.

## Timetable
The provisional timetable is published four weeks before the exams, and the final timetable two weeks before. If two of your papers clash (common for backlog subjects), report it to the Examination Cell within 3 days of the provisional timetable.

## Hall ticket (admit card)
Hall tickets are released on the exam portal 7 days before the exams for students who meet the attendance and fee requirements. If a subject or subject code is wrong on your hall ticket, report it to the Examination Cell immediately with a screenshot.

## Rules
Non-programmable calculators are allowed unless the paper states otherwise. Students must be seated 15 minutes before the start.
