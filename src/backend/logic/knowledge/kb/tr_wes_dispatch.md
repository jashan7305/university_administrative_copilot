# Sending Transcripts Directly to WES, ECE or a Foreign University

Many credential evaluation agencies (WES, ECE, IQAS) and foreign universities accept transcripts only when sent directly by the university in a sealed envelope.

## Procedure
1. Create your WES or university application and note the reference number.
2. Fill form EX-11 and select "Direct dispatch". Attach the WES academic records request form, already filled with your details.
3. Pay the transcript fee and international courier charges of Rs. 2,500 per destination.
4. The Records Section seals, signs and couriers the documents within 10 working days and shares the tracking number by email.

## Notes
Electronic submission through WES is available for this university. Select "Electronic" on the form to avoid courier charges. Status can be tracked on the examination portal.
