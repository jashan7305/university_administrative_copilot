# New ID Card, Delays and Renewal

## First issue
ID cards for newly admitted students are printed from the photographs uploaded during admission and distributed through the department within 3 weeks of the start of the semester.

## Delay
If you have not received your card after 3 weeks, check the status on the student portal (ID Card > Status). If the status shows "Photo pending", upload a photograph. Otherwise, contact the ID Card Cell with your admission number.

## Annual renewal
The card is validated each academic year after fee payment. The validity sticker is issued by the department office. Expired cards do not work at the library or at campus gates.

## Forgotten card
Students who forget their card can obtain a one-day gate pass from the security office by showing the digital ID on the student app.
