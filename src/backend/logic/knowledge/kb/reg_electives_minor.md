# Electives, Open Electives, Minor and Honours Programmes

## Electives and prerequisites
Each elective lists its prerequisites in the course catalogue on the ERP. Students who have not passed the prerequisites cannot register. Seats are allotted on CGPA when an elective is over-subscribed, and the others are waitlisted.

## Open electives
Open electives from other departments can be taken from semester 5. Up to two open electives count towards the degree.

## Minor and honours
A minor (18 extra credits in another discipline) and an honours degree (18 extra credits in your own discipline) are open to students with a CGPA of 7.5 or above and no backlogs, from semester 3. Apply on the ERP during registration.

## Summer term
Summer courses are offered for backlog clearance and acceleration. Registration opens in May.

## Project and thesis credits
Final-year project or thesis credits are registered like a course, after a supervisor is allotted by the department.
