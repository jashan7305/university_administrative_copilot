# Fee Payment, Deadlines, Installments and Late Fees

## How to pay
Semester fees (tuition, development, lab, library and exam fees) are paid through the student portal by UPI, net banking, debit or credit card, or NEFT to the university account in the fee challan. Demand drafts in favour of "The Registrar" are accepted at the Accounts Office. Cash is not accepted above Rs. 10,000.

## Deadlines and late fee
Fees are due within 15 days of the start of the semester. A late fee of Rs. 100 per day applies after that, up to Rs. 3,000. Students with unpaid fees cannot register for exams.

## Installments
Students facing financial difficulty can apply to the Accounts Office for payment in two installments, with a recommendation from their HoD. The second installment is due before the mid-semester exams.

## Fee structure document
A fee structure letter for education loans can be downloaded from the portal (Fees > Documents) or requested from the Accounts Office.
