# Government Scholarships

Students from SC, ST, OBC, EBC and minority communities, and girl students under specific schemes, can apply for central and state government scholarships and freeships (full fee waivers).

## Procedure
1. Register on the National Scholarship Portal or the state scholarship portal during the application window (usually August to October).
2. Upload the caste certificate, income certificate, previous marksheet, domicile certificate and fee receipt. The bank account must be linked to Aadhaar.
3. Submit a printout to the Scholarship Cell for institute verification before the institute deadline.
4. The government disburses the amount directly to the bank account.

## Delays and rejections
If your scholarship is not credited, check the application status on the portal. Common reasons for rejection are a mismatch between the Aadhaar and bank name, an expired income certificate or a missing institute verification. Applications can be corrected in the "defective" window announced by the portal.
