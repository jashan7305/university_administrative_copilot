# Branch Change, Semester Withdrawal and Re-registration

## Branch change
Students may apply to change their branch (major) after the first year on form AR-03. Seats are allotted on first-year CGPA (minimum 8.0, no backlogs), subject to vacancies in the requested branch.

## Semester withdrawal
Students who must discontinue for medical or personal reasons may withdraw from a semester on form AR-06 with parent consent before the mid-semester exams. Withdrawn semesters do not count towards the maximum duration.

## Re-registration after a break
Students returning after a withdrawal or break re-register at the Academic Office before the semester starts. The re-registration fee is Rs. 2,000, and the curriculum in force at that time applies.

## New students
New students complete admission registration by verifying their documents at the Academic Office and activating their ERP account within the first week.
