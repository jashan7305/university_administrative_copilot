# Supplementary, Backlog (ATKT), Re-exam and Makeup Examinations

## Failed a subject
Students who fail a subject (a backlog or ATKT) can reappear in the supplementary examination held within six weeks of the results, or in the next regular end semester examination. Register on the exam portal and pay Rs. 800 per paper. Internal marks are carried forward.

## Missed an exam because of illness or emergency
A makeup (re-exam) is granted only for documented medical emergencies, hospitalisation, bereavement in the immediate family, or representing the university in approved events. Apply within 3 days of the missed paper with a medical certificate from a registered practitioner, countersigned by the university medical officer.

## Improvement exams
Students who passed but want to improve their grade may appear once per subject in the supplementary exam. The better of the two grades is retained.
