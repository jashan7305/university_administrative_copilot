# Maintenance Complaints, Vacating the Hostel and Refunds

## Maintenance complaints
Problems with the water heater (geyser), fans, lights, taps, beds, door locks or wifi should be raised as a ticket on the hostel app. Most complaints are resolved within 48 hours. If a complaint is not resolved, escalate it to the warden. Mess food complaints go to the mess committee through the same app.

## Roommate issues
Roommate conflicts are handled by the floor warden first, then by the chief warden.

## Vacating and no dues
Before leaving the hostel, submit form HS-09. The warden checks the room inventory and issues the hostel no dues certificate, which is needed for your degree, migration and transfer certificates.

## Refunds
The security deposit is refunded within 30 days of vacating. Mess fees are refunded pro rata if you leave mid-semester with at least 30 days' notice. Hostel rent is not refundable after the first month.
