# Duplicate Marksheet / Grade Card and CGPA Conversion Certificate

## Duplicate marksheet or grade card
If a semester marksheet (grade card or mark statement) is lost or damaged, apply on form EX-12. Lost marksheets need an FIR or police lost report. Damaged ones must be surrendered. The fee is Rs. 500 per marksheet and it is issued in 7 working days.

## Consolidated marksheet
A consolidated marksheet listing all semesters on one page is available after the final semester, on the same form.

## CGPA to percentage conversion certificate
Many employers and foreign universities ask for a percentage. The university issues a CGPA conversion certificate stating the official formula (percentage = CGPA x 10 - 7.5 for this university's grading scheme) for Rs. 200.
