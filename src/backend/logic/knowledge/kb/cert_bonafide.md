# Bonafide Certificate

A bonafide certificate confirms that you are a currently enrolled student of the university. It is commonly required for bank and education loans, passport and visa applications, scholarship forms, internships, railway concessions and hostel or rental agreements.

## Eligibility
Any student with an active enrolment for the current academic year. Students with pending fee dues must clear them first.

## Procedure
1. Log in to the student portal and open Services > Certificates > Bonafide, or collect form SS-01 from the Student Section.
2. Select the purpose (for example bank loan or visa); the purpose is printed on the certificate.
3. Upload or attach your ID card and current fee receipt and pay the fee of Rs. 50 online.
4. The certificate is issued within 2 working days and can be collected from the Student Section between 10:00 and 16:00, or downloaded as a digitally signed PDF.

## Notes
Urgent same-day requests are accepted before 12:00 for an additional Rs. 100, subject to approval by the Assistant Registrar. A separate certificate is needed for each purpose.
