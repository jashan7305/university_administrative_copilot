# No Objection Certificate (NOC) and Character / Conduct Certificate

A No Objection Certificate is issued when a student needs the university's permission for an external activity: internships during the semester, participation in competitions, part-time work, passport applications or exchange programmes. A character (conduct) certificate attests to the student's conduct and is often required for jobs, scholarships and higher studies.

## Procedure
1. For an NOC, attach the offer or invitation letter that states the dates of the activity.
2. Obtain a recommendation from your faculty advisor or Head of Department on form SS-02.
3. Submit the form on the portal and pay Rs. 100.
4. The certificate is issued in 3 working days.

## Notes
Internship NOCs during teaching weeks are issued only if attendance is not affected, or if the internship is a credited course. Character certificates are issued to current students and to alumni within two years of graduation.
