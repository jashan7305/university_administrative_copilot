# Official Transcript

An official transcript is a consolidated record of all courses, credits and grades across all semesters, signed by the Controller of Examinations and sealed with the university stamp. It is required for admission to master's and PhD programmes, for employers abroad, and for credential evaluation agencies.

## Procedure
1. Apply on the examination portal (Services > Transcripts) and upload copies of all semester grade cards.
2. Choose the number of copies and whether each should be in a sealed envelope.
3. Pay Rs. 1,000 for the first copy and Rs. 200 for each additional copy.
4. Transcripts are ready in 10 working days and can be collected from the Records Section or posted.

## Notes
Unofficial transcripts (a grade history printout) can be downloaded instantly from the student portal but are not accepted by most universities. Each sealed envelope is signed across the flap.
