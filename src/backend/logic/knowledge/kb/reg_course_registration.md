# Semester Course Registration, Add/Drop and Late Registration

## Registration
Course registration for the next semester opens on the ERP 10 days before the semester starts. Select your core courses and electives. Your faculty advisor must approve the registration on the ERP. Registrations pending advisor approval are not final.

## Credit limits
Students must register for 16 to 26 credits per semester. More than 26 credits needs a CGPA of 8.0 and the Dean's approval.

## Add/drop
Courses can be added or dropped in the first two weeks of the semester through the ERP. After that, a course can only be withdrawn (a W grade) until the mid-semester exams.

## Late registration
Students who miss the window (including because of portal errors) can register in the first week with a late fee of Rs. 1,000. Portal errors reported with a screenshot during the window are exempt from the fee.

## Backlogs
Students with backlogs must still register each semester and include backlog courses first.
