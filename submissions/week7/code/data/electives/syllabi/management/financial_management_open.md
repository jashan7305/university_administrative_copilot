Course Outline

Course Code: OE-509
Course Title: Financial Management for Engineers
Course Type: Open Elective
Program(s): All UG programmes
Year / Semester: III / V
Credits: 3 (Lecture 3, Practical 0, Tutorial 0)
Prerequisites: Principles of Economics and Management

Detailed Syllabus
Unit 1  Financial statements and ratio analysis.
Unit 2  Time value of money; capital budgeting (NPV, IRR, payback).
Unit 3  Cost of capital and capital structure.
Unit 4  Working capital management.
Unit 5  Financing a technology venture.
