New open elective (proposed): Sustainable Engineering and ESG Fundamentals

Open to undergraduate students of all MPSTME programmes from Semester V onward (taken in Semester V or VI).
3 credits, 3 lecture hours a week. No prerequisites.

The course introduces sustainability for engineers: life-cycle assessment, carbon accounting (scope 1, 2 and 3
emissions), circular economy and design for disassembly, energy efficiency, environmental, social and governance
(ESG) reporting frameworks (BRSR, GRI), and the business case for sustainable products. Students carry out a
life-cycle assessment of a product from their own discipline.
