NEW COURSE PROPOSAL (Board of Studies, for introduction in 2028-29)

Generative AI and Large Language Model Applications

This new 3-credit course is proposed as a programme elective for third-year (Semester VI) students of
B.Tech. Computer Science, B.Tech. Computer Engineering and B.Tech. Artificial Intelligence. It will be taught as
2 hours of lectures and 2 hours of laboratory work per week.

Students joining the course are expected to have completed Machine Learning and Data Structures and Algorithms.

The course teaches students to build applications on top of large language models. Topics: the transformer
architecture and pre-training; prompting and in-context learning; retrieval-augmented generation over a
document collection; fine-tuning with parameter-efficient methods (LoRA); evaluating generated text and detecting
hallucinations; agents and tool use; safety, bias and responsible deployment of generative AI. Students complete a
team project that ships a small LLM application with an evaluation report.
