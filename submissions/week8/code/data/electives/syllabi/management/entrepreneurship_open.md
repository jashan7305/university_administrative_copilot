Course Outline

Course Code: OE-612
Course Title: Entrepreneurship and New Venture Creation
Course Type: Open Elective
Program(s): All UG programmes
Year / Semester: III / VI
Credits: 3 (Lecture 3, Practical 0, Tutorial 0)
Prerequisites: None

Detailed Syllabus
Unit 1  The entrepreneurial mindset; opportunity recognition; innovation.
Unit 2  Customer discovery and the lean startup; business model canvas.
Unit 3  Managing innovation: product-market fit, minimum viable product, pivoting.
Unit 4  Funding: bootstrapping, angel and venture capital, government schemes.
Unit 5  Pitching and scaling a venture.
