GROUP 10 — University Administrative Copilot (Roll No. J054 J055 J056 J057 J059)

Problem:
    Students and faculty ask administrative questions in natural language, while institutional processes are often
    distributed across documents and systems.

Objective:
Build an NLP component capable of understanding and routing administrative requests.

Example:
    “I lost my ID card. What should I do?”

    Output:
        Intent: ID Card Replacement
        Department: Academic Administration
        Required Documents: ...
        Next Action: Submit replacement request

Expected NLP Components:
    intent classification
    NER
    information extraction
    semantic search
    RAG

Possible Intents:
    certificates
    transcripts
    ID cards
    hostel
    examinations
    fees
    scholarships
    attendance
    academic registration

Stretch Goal:
    Automatically generate a structured service request from the student's message.
